This guide provides best practices for safely and effectively participating in TCP staking.

## Security Best Practices

### Wallet Security

✅ **Use a secure wallet**
- Hardware wallets (Ledger, Trezor) for large stakes
- Reputable software wallets (MetaMask) for smaller amounts
- Never use exchange wallets for staking

✅ **Protect your private keys**
- Never share your private keys or seed phrases
- Store seed phrases securely offline
- Use hardware wallets for additional security

✅ **Enable security features**
- Use strong passwords
- Enable two-factor authentication where available
- Keep wallet software updated

✅ **Verify addresses**
- Always verify contract addresses on PolygonScan
- Double-check addresses before transactions
- Use address bookmarks to avoid typos

### Transaction Security

✅ **Verify before confirming**
- Check transaction details carefully
- Verify the contract address
- Confirm the amount and recipient
- Review gas prices

✅ **Use appropriate gas prices**
- Check current gas prices on PolygonScan
- Use standard or fast gas for normal transactions
- Avoid extremely high gas prices unless urgent

✅ **Monitor transactions**
- Wait for transaction confirmation
- Verify on PolygonScan after confirmation
- Keep records of all transactions

✅ **Avoid phishing**
- Never click links from unknown sources
- Verify URLs before entering sensitive information
- Be cautious of social engineering attempts

## Operational Best Practices

### Before Staking

✅ **Understand the mechanism**
- Read the documentation thoroughly
- Understand how rewards are calculated
- Know the risks involved

✅ **Verify the contract**
- Check the contract address on PolygonScan
- Verify the source code
- Confirm the deployment transaction

✅ **Start small**
- Test with a small amount first
- Verify the process works as expected
- Increase stake gradually

✅ **Have sufficient MATIC**
- Ensure you have MATIC for gas fees
- Account for multiple transactions
- Keep a buffer for unexpected fees

### During Staking

✅ **Monitor your position**
- Check your stake balance regularly
- Track your earned rewards
- Verify transactions on PolygonScan

✅ **Claim rewards periodically**
- Claim rewards regularly to compound
- Don't let rewards accumulate indefinitely
- Track your claiming history

✅ **Stay informed**
- Follow protocol updates
- Monitor governance announcements
- Stay aware of reward rate changes

✅ **Keep records**
- Document all transactions
- Track stake amounts and dates
- Record reward claims for tax purposes

### When Claiming Rewards

✅ **Check available rewards**
- Verify you have accrued rewards
- Check the reward pool status
- Ensure sufficient contract balance

✅ **Verify gas prices**
- Check current gas prices
- Use appropriate gas settings
- Confirm transaction details

✅ **Confirm transactions**
- Wait for confirmation
- Verify on PolygonScan
- Check your wallet balance

✅ **Verify receipt**
- Confirm tokens received
- Check your reward balance
- Verify on PolygonScan

### When Unstaking

✅ **Claim rewards first**
- Claim pending rewards before unstaking
- Ensure you don't lose rewards
- Verify rewards are in your wallet

✅ **Verify unstake amount**
- Double-check the amount
- Confirm you want to unstake
- Review remaining stake

✅ **Understand no lock-up**
- Remember tokens return immediately
- Plan for liquidity needs
- Consider long-term strategy

✅ **Confirm tokens received**
- Verify tokens in your wallet
- Check on PolygonScan
- Update your records

## Risk Management

### Diversification

✅ **Don't stake everything**
- Keep some tokens for other uses
- Maintain emergency liquidity
- Diversify across multiple positions

✅ **Gradual staking**
- Stake gradually over time
- Test with small amounts first
- Increase stake as you gain confidence

✅ **Monitor market conditions**
- Be aware of market volatility
- Understand token price risk
- Consider your risk tolerance

### Loss Prevention

✅ **Backup your wallet**
- Keep secure backups of seed phrases
- Store backups in multiple locations
- Test recovery procedures

✅ **Verify addresses**
- Always verify contract addresses
- Use address bookmarks
- Double-check before transactions

✅ **Avoid common mistakes**
- Don't send tokens to wrong addresses
- Don't approve unlimited amounts
- Don't share private keys

### Emergency Procedures

✅ **Know how to unstake**
- Understand the unstaking process
- Know there's no lock-up period
- Be prepared to unstake quickly if needed

✅ **Have a recovery plan**
- Know how to recover your wallet
- Have backup seed phrases
- Test recovery procedures

✅ **Report issues**
- Report bugs to the protocol team
- Don't exploit vulnerabilities
- Follow responsible disclosure

## Staking Strategy

### Conservative Approach

**For risk-averse users:**
- Stake a small percentage of holdings
- Claim rewards frequently
- Maintain large emergency reserves
- Monitor closely

### Moderate Approach

**For balanced users:**
- Stake a reasonable percentage
- Claim and compound periodically
- Maintain adequate reserves
- Monitor regularly

### Aggressive Approach

**For experienced users:**
- Stake a large percentage
- Compound rewards frequently
- Minimize reserves
- Monitor actively

❌ **Don't send tokens to wrong addresses**

❌ **Don't approve unlimited amounts**
- Approve only what you need
- Revoke approvals when done
- Monitor your approvals

❌ **Don't ignore gas prices**
- Check gas before transactions
- Use appropriate gas settings
- Avoid overpaying for gas
