This page documents important risks and considerations for TCP staking. Understanding these risks is essential before participating.

## Smart Contract Risks

### Contract Vulnerability Risk

**Risk**
- Staking contract may have vulnerabilities
- Vulnerabilities could lead to loss of funds
- Audits reduce but don't eliminate risk

**Mitigation**
- TCPStakingV2 underwent internal audits
- Code reviewed and tested
- Transparent code on PolygonScan
- Comprehensive Mainnet validation

**What You Can Do**
- Review contract code on PolygonScan
- Understand the mechanism
- Start with small amount
- Monitor for issues

### Integration Risk

**Risk**
- Integration with other contracts may have issues
- Complex interactions may have bugs
- Unexpected behavior possible

**Mitigation**
- Thorough testing completed
- Mainnet validation performed
- Community monitoring active
- Issue response procedures in place

**What You Can Do**
- Test with small amount
- Monitor for issues
- Report problems responsibly
- Stay informed of updates

## Market Risks

### Token Price Risk

**Risk**
- Token value may decrease
- Rewards may not offset losses
- Market volatility possible

**Mitigation**
- Understand tokenomics
- Monitor market conditions
- Diversify holdings
- Take long-term perspective

**What You Can Do**
- Understand token value drivers
- Diversify portfolio
- Take long-term view
- Monitor price trends

### Liquidity Risk

**Risk**
- Tokens locked while staking
- May need tokens for other uses
- Opportunity cost possible

**Mitigation**
- No lock-up period
- Unstake anytime
- Instant unstaking available
- Flexible participation

**What You Can Do**
- Only stake tokens you can lock
- Plan for liquidity needs
- Maintain emergency fund
- Unstake if needed

### Reward Rate Risk

**Risk**
- Reward rate may decrease
- Rewards may become unsustainable
- Rate changes possible

**Mitigation**
- Transparent rate management
- Sustainable design
- Community input
- Advance notice of changes

**What You Can Do**
- Monitor reward rate
- Assess sustainability
- Provide feedback
- Stay informed

## Risk Assessment

### High Risk Factors

- Cryptocurrency market volatility
- Smart contract vulnerabilities
- Regulatory uncertainty
- Operational disruptions

- Reward rate changes
- Market competition
- Integration issues
- Community governance

- Double-payment prevention (reward reservation)
- Owner validation (multisig governance)
- Balance verification (continuous validation)

## Emergency Procedures

### If You Suspect an Issue

1. **Don't panic**, Take time to assess
2. **Verify information**, Check official sources
3. **Report responsibly**, Contact protocol team
4. **Document details**, Record what happened
5. **Seek advice**, Consult professionals

### If You Need to Unstake Quickly

1. **Access staking interface**, Connect your wallet
2. **Click unstake**, Enter amount to unstake
3. **Confirm transaction**, Sign in your wallet
4. **Wait for confirmation**, Verify on PolygonScan
5. **Check wallet**, Verify tokens received
